- Q1 (data model, D17): Does "Autonomía" count correct escalations, or only invoices the agent closed alone (PAGAR/FRENAR)? And is it measured on the first agent decision or the latest? - Q2 (missing company context, S-Policy): what does the "duplicate window" bound? Provisional (R-06): the look-back of the exact-duplicate check (same beneficiary + same number). Should duplicates be caught at any age instead?
- Q3 (missing company context, S-Escalation): may a human approve a payment that exceeds the period budget, the account balance or the per-payment limit? Provisional (R-21): no; they change the policy (new version) and re-evaluate.
- Q5 (architecture, S-Auth vs D2; 17 Oct, QA on Task 2): with the public demo login, a member could write a PAGAR outside the rules straight into the database through Supabase REST. Closing that means the Worker writes decisions, approval tokens/uses and agent runs with the service-role client from one more module (`src/agent/record.ts`), beyond S-Auth's "one module used by one handler" (already widened to the cron by R-23). Provisional (R-32, R-33): yes. Members lose write access to those four tables, and the database refuses any PAGAR whose checks break the rules. Is that acceptable?
- Q4 (data model, S-DataSet): "COP invoice whose USD equivalent exceeds the limit": the per-payment limit (FRENAR) or the approval threshold (ESCALAR, as PLAN seeds it)? Provisional (R-07): per-payment limit, threshold case kept as an extra.
- Q7 (architecture, Cloudflare plan; 17 Oct, first deploy): the Cloudflare account is on Workers Free, which allows 50 subrequests per invocation, and one agent run over 29 invoices needs about 120. Upgrading to Workers Paid (USD 5/month, 1000 subrequests) is your call and costs money. Provisional (R-56..R-60, AC-70): stay on Free. A run decides what fits and the browser continues it until done. The cron rotates companies. On a paid plan one constant can be raised. Do you want to upgrade?
- Q8 (missing company context, Cloudflare plan / Workers AI; 17 Oct, final QA): the invoice reader runs on Workers AI. The free allocation is 10,000 "neurons" a day, about 250 invoice reads, and it resets at 00:00 UTC (19:00 Bogotá). Our own testing used it up on 17 Oct. After that, every newly pasted or uploaded invoice could not be read until the reset. Workers Paid (USD 5/month, Q7) lifts the cap; reads beyond the free 10,000 neurons are billed per use, about USD 0.011 per 1,000 neurons, so roughly 0.04 US cents per read. That is your money and your call. Provisional (R-75, R-76): stay on Free. When the reader is unavailable, the invoice is still stored and escalated as «factura sin leer: el lector de facturas no estaba disponible; vuelve a ingresarla más tarde». It is never paid, and it is never called «ilegible». Seeded invoices never need the reader, so `/demo` and every decision work regardless. Live reads during the run are rationed (QA one per channel, the video one upload), and README states the daily limit. Do you want to upgrade so judges never meet the limit?
- Q9 (missing company context, AC-14; 17 Oct, review of Task 7d): do the company's vendors print retenciones (ReteFuente, ReteIVA, ReteICA) or descuentos inside the invoice total, and when they do, is the amount to pay the gross total or the net? Provisional (R-81): neither is treated as an adjustment, so such an invoice escalates with «el total no cuadra» and a person answers it. The guard never guesses a sign convention that could turn a wrong total into a PAGAR. Should withholdings be recognised?
- Q6 (data model, D5; 17 Oct, Task 3): an invoice escalated *because* it would exceed the budget is itself "pending ESCALATED", so under D5 it stays in "comprometido". Committed is then above budget for the rest of the month, and every later invoice and every approval fails the budget check until a person rejects something or raises the budget. Provisional (R-35): keep D5 literally, which is the conservative reading. The happy-path video rejects the "cuenta cambiada" invoice before approving Nube Sur. Should budget-blocked invoices stop counting as committed instead?
